6. Mathematical Formula
T_{Annual\_Mean} = \frac{1}{12} \sum_{m=1}^{12} \overline{T}_m
7. Calculation Steps 1. Calculate 30-year climatological mean for each month (Jan-Dec). 2. Sum the 12 monthly means. 3. Divide by 12.

9. Worked Numerical Example Monthly means [12.0, 13.5, 17.0, 21.5, 26.0, 29.5, 31.0, 31.0, 28.0, 24.0, 18.5, 14.0] -> Sum = 246.0 -> Mean = 20.5 °C.
10. Scientific Interpretation Baseline thermal state of the atmosphere, fundamental for ecological classification, energy demand, and comfort.
